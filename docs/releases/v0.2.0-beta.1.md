# Talkboard public beta 0.2.0

A non-personalized iPad AAC demo with four communication pages, visible page
navigation, caregiver-protected editing and a separate ABC / 123 activity.

- Illustrated generic home and vocabulary pages, with credited symbols and a
  stock water photo. The People page starts empty for users to personalize.
- Tappable letters and numbers 1-20 with quantity dots; Home stays accessible.
- Improved interruption and foreground audio recovery.
- Separate public app identity, Talkboard Demo. Existing public saves are preserved.

Known limitations: letter pronunciation needs improvement and audible checking
(A/E issues; G adjustment unconfirmed). Song and guided counting playback are
not included. Extra vocabulary pages are transient. General export/restore,
printable backup, recording UI and physical idle-audio acceptance remain open.

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Mulberry CC BY-SA 4.0 and Pexels. The complete demo includes

Build from source with XcodeGen and Xcode, or use the attached simulator app on
a Mac with a compatible iPad simulator. The simulator ZIP does not install on
a physical iPad. Physical builds require your own Apple signing setup; no signed
household IPA or provisioning material is distributed.

No personal boards, photos, recordings or private history are included.
Software verification is not evidence of communication or clinical benefit.

Verification: 236 Core, native and UI tests passed on an iPad simulator.
The unsigned Release simulator build passed for arm64 and x86_64.
